Bible reader

The Bible app needs audio files on the SD card. Place each translation in its
own folder under /bible. For example, use /bible/NLT Bible/01 - Genesis/ and
put files named Faith Comes By Hearing - Genesis 1.mp3, Genesis 2.mp3, and so
on in that book folder. When Bible Reader is selected in the Builder, its setup
guide and sorting scripts download to the Builder workspace's help folder.
Choose Bible, then a version, book, and chapter. Space plays or pauses.
Option+H reads controls.
