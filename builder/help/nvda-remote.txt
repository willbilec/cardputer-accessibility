NVDA Remote

Configure a session in NVDA Remote Access on the Windows computer. On the
Cardputer, choose a saved session, load nvda-remote.ini from the SD card, or
enter the connection details. The session key stays silent. Enter connects;
Option+Enter accepts a controlled computer. Fn sends NVDA commands when Caps
Lock is enabled as an NVDA modifier. Option+K reads controls. Alt+Q returns Home.
